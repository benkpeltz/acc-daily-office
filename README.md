# ACC Daily Office

> **Daily Prayer for the Anglican Church of Canada**  
> Featuring both the **Book of Alternative Services (1985)** and the **Book of Common Prayer (1962 Canada)**.

**Live Web Application:** [https://benkpeltz.github.io/acc-daily-office/](https://benkpeltz.github.io/acc-daily-office/)

---

## About the App

**ACC Daily Office** is a free, mobile-friendly, offline-capable Daily Office web application designed for personal devotion, small groups, and parish worship across the Anglican Church of Canada.

It brings together Canada's two authorized liturgical traditions into a single, intuitive reader. Whether you pray contemporary liturgies or traditional Cranmerian offices, you can switch between the **Book of Alternative Services** and the **1962 Book of Common Prayer** with a single tap.

---

## What’s Included

### 1. The Book of Alternative Services (BAS 1985)
* **Morning Prayer, Evening Prayer, & Compline:** Full contemporary daily liturgies with proper antiphons, versicles, and collects.
* **Inclusive Language Edition:** Switch between the General Synod 2019 *Inclusive Language Liturgical Psalter & Canticles* and the original 1985 BAS text.
* **The Canticles:** Seasonal canticles including the *Venite* (Psalm 95), the *Benedictus* (Song of Zechariah), the *Phos Hilaron* (O Gracious Light), the *Magnificat* (Song of Mary), and the *Nunc Dimittis* (Song of Simeon).
* **The Lord’s Prayer:** Choose between contemporary (*"Our Father in heaven..."*) and traditional language.

### 2. The Book of Common Prayer (BCP 1962 Canada)
* **Mattins (Morning Prayer):** Traditional Cranmerian English (*thee/thou*), opening sentences of Scripture, the BCP General Confession and Absolution, Preces (*"O Lord, open thou our lips"*), the *Te Deum Laudamus*, and BCP *Benedictus*.
* **Evensong (Evening Prayer):** Traditional choral and spoken Evensong structure featuring the classic *Magnificat* and *Nunc Dimittis*, classic suffrages (*"O Lord, save the King"*, *"Give peace in our time, O Lord"*), and the Third Collect for Aid against all Perils (*"Lighten our darkness, we beseech thee, O Lord"*).
* **Coverdale Psalter (1535/1962):** The beloved Miles Coverdale translation pointed with traditional colon (`:`) chant pauses and BCP *Gloria Patri*.
* **An Order for Compline:** The traditional 1962 BCP night office (*"Turn us then, O God our Saviour"*).

## Built-In Liturgical Tools

* **Automated Calendar & Seasons:** Calculates the moveable feasts (Ash Wednesday, Easter, Ascension, Pentecost, Trinity Sunday) and seasons (Advent, Christmas, Epiphany, Lent, Holy Week, Eastertide, and Ordinary Time) for any year, complete with liturgical colour indicators.
* **Two-Year Daily Office Lectionary:** Automatically rotates between Year One and Year Two on the First Sunday of Advent.
* **Direct Scripture Links:** Formatted 1-tap links open the appointed daily readings directly in the New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition (NRSVue) on Bible Gateway and Oremus.
* **Customizable Reader Controls:**
  * **Themes:** Clean Light, glare-free Warm Sepia, and Night Dark.
  * **Typography:** Adjustable prayer text size slider (15px to 25px).
  * **Rubrics:** Toggle liturgical instructions in red on or off.
* **Recurring Calendar Schedule (.ics):** Generate and download a recurring prayer reminder schedule for Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook with direct links to the app.

## Installing on Your Phone or Tablet (Offline PWA)

This app is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it on your device for a full-screen, native app experience that works **100% offline**:

### iPhone & iPad (iOS)
1. Open **[https://benkpeltz.github.io/acc-daily-office/](https://benkpeltz.github.io/acc-daily-office/)** in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square icon with the upward arrow at the bottom).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm the name (**ACC Office**) and tap **Add**. The app icon will appear on your home screen.

### Android
1. Open **[https://benkpeltz.github.io/acc-daily-office/](https://benkpeltz.github.io/acc-daily-office/)** in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner (or tap the **Install App** / **Add to Home screen** banner).
3. Tap **Install** to add the app icon to your home screen or app drawer.

### Mac, Windows, or Chromebook
1. Open the link in **Chrome**, **Edge**, or **Safari**.
2. Click the **Install** icon on the right side of the URL address bar (or go to browser settings > **Install ACC Daily Office**).
